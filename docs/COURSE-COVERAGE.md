# Published course coverage

Sarh contains two Semester 7 subjects in Arabic and English. Each journey combines an introductory chapter, prerequisite refreshers, a guided story map, practice and flashcards.

| Subject | Journeys | Story steps | Practice questions | Flashcards | Checked source solutions |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Theory of Structures II | 12 | 75 | 165 | 170 | 76 |
| Engineering Economics | 15 | 75 | 225 | 225 | 51 |
| Total | 27 | 150 | 390 | 395 | 127 |

The library includes 35 structural schematics, 18 cash-flow timelines and 74 exact filename/page fallbacks. The 38 optional video resources were checked for identity, availability and relevance; videos require internet. No exam-percentage coverage is claimed.

Structures II covers slope-deflection and moment distribution for beams and frames, influence lines and structural models. Engineering Economics maps all six supplied lecture chapters: engineering decisions and costs, markets, the lecturer's interest/riba context, interest factors and series, rates and continuous flows, depreciation, and project comparison. Supplementary journeys cover supplied FE/PE practice, uncertainty and inventory.

**Lecture curriculum coverage is distinct from exhaustive archival problem coverage.** The external Structures II register has 37 unresolved entries beyond its 76 checked solutions. The Economics collection contains 16 PDFs and 840 pages; its 51 published solutions are a checked subset, not every unique archival question. Additional scan inspection, deduplication and independent solutions remain necessary before claiming the full archive is solved.

Only finished explanations, final problem data, checked calculations, original instructional sketches and filename/page metadata are distributed. Original PDFs, OCR, rendered source pages, private authoring inputs, credentials and personal notebook data are excluded.

Run `npm run content:check` for current counts and teaching/practice/recall coverage; `npm run release:check` and the production build check source exclusions. `npm test` checks numerical examples, storage, timer behaviour, the local launcher and asset caching. The economics fixtures in `tests/fixtures` are final numerical validation data, not raw source extraction.
